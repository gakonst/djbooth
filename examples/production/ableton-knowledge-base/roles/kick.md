# Kick

Create a tuned, short low-frequency anchor with a controlled transient and enough upper information to read on small systems.

## Build order

1. Choose or synthesize the body in the track key/register.
2. Set pitch and amplitude envelopes before effects.
3. Add only the click or harmonics needed for translation.
4. Control tail length against the bass rhythm.
5. Check phase, headroom, and mono.

## Listen for

- One clear transient
- Stable fundamental
- No tail collision with bass
- Audibility at low playback level

## Devices

- **DrumCell** (partial; 7 evidence videos): Ableton's native single-voice drum-synthesis engine for tuned electronic drum design.
- **Operator** (partial; 46 evidence videos): A four-oscillator FM/additive/subtractive synth suited to precise bass, percussion, keys, and metallic timbres.
- **OriginalSimpler** (complete; 49 evidence videos): A focused sample instrument for classic, one-shot, slicing, and warped sample playback.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **DrumBuss** (partial; 24 evidence videos): An integrated drum color, transient, compression, and low-frequency enhancement processor.
- **Saturator** (partial; 52 evidence videos): A focused waveshaping stage for harmonic density, clipping, warmth, and peak control.

## Principles and recipes

- Principles: mono-foundation-wide-detail, distortion-by-band, dynamics-follow-groove, drums-need-separated-stages
- Recipes: kickAuthority, kickFocus
