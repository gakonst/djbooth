# Hats and percussion

Provide subdivision, syncopation, and high-frequency motion without constant harsh energy.

## Build order

1. Establish one clear subdivision role.
2. Vary velocity and articulation within narrow bounds.
3. Add syncopated answers and occasional omissions.
4. Control harsh resonances.
5. Reserve spatial or glitch gestures for phrase punctuation.

## Listen for

- Groove at low volume
- Air without pain
- Different phrase endings
- No stereo distraction from the core pulse

## Devices

- **Collision** (complete; 16 evidence videos): A physical-modeling percussion synthesizer built from mallets, resonators, and material behavior.
- **OriginalSimpler** (complete; 49 evidence videos): A focused sample instrument for classic, one-shot, slicing, and warped sample playback.
- **DrumGroupDevice** (partial; 50 evidence videos): Represent Ableton's serial, parallel, layered, return, selector, and macro graph structure.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **AutoPan2** (partial; 0 evidence videos): Animate pitch, phase, level, or stereo position.
- **BeatRepeat** (complete; 9 evidence videos): A synchronized buffer repeater for fills, stutters, and controlled rhythmic disruption.
- **GrainDelay** (partial; 11 evidence videos): A granular delay for pitch-shifted repeats, smears, glitches, and resampled ear candy.
