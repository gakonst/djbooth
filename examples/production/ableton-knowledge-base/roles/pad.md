# Pads and harmonic beds

Support harmony and depth with evolving tone while leaving room for foreground, bass, and drums.

## Build order

1. Choose voicings that avoid the bass register.
2. Use slow envelope and low-depth modulation for evolution.
3. Remove masking mids or lows in context.
4. Spread upper detail while controlling center energy.
5. Automate density and brightness by section.

## Listen for

- Harmony is clear but not foreground
- No low-mid fog
- Motion is slower than lead articulation
- Section transitions change voicing or density

## Devices

- **InstrumentMeld** (partial; 14 evidence videos): A dual-engine macro-oscillator synth for complex timbral blending and modulation.
- **UltraAnalog** (complete; 79 evidence videos): Ableton's analog-modeling subtractive synthesizer family.
- **MultiSampler** (complete; 45 evidence videos): Ableton's multisample playback family for velocity, key, and sample-zone instruments.
- **Chorus2** (partial; 62 evidence videos): Animate pitch, phase, level, or stereo position.
- **Hybrid** (complete; 27 evidence videos): Ableton's convolution/algorithmic hybrid reverb for realistic spaces and designed tails.
- **Reverb** (partial; 145 evidence videos): Place sound in depth or create time-based rhythmic structure.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **StereoGain** (partial; 50 evidence videos): Ableton's utility/gain family for level, width, channel, phase, and low-frequency stereo control.
