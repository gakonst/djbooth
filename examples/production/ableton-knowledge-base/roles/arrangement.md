# Arrangement and development

Transform loops into sections through role changes, motif development, density, register, and transitions.

## Build order

1. Define the energy and active roles of each section.
2. Write at least two motif/bass/drum states.
3. Change orchestration or register before adding transition FX.
4. Use fills, automation, and resampled gestures to connect states.
5. Audit the full-song energy curve without soloing.

## Listen for

- Every 8–16 bars changes meaningful information
- Hooks have answers and absences
- Transitions predict the next section
- The climax is earned by prior restraint
