# Texture and ear candy

Create sparse, curated details that reward attention and mark transitions without becoming a continuous noise layer.

## Build order

1. Generate from existing motif or drum material.
2. Print unstable processing to audio.
3. Curate the strongest short fragments.
4. Place them in structural gaps or boundaries.
5. Filter, pan, and level them behind primary roles.

## Listen for

- Details are discoverable, not dominant
- Each gesture has a structural reason
- Repeated renders stay deterministic
- No cumulative high-frequency clutter

## Devices

- **GrainDelay** (partial; 11 evidence videos): A granular delay for pitch-shifted repeats, smears, glitches, and resampled ear candy.
- **BeatRepeat** (complete; 9 evidence videos): A synchronized buffer repeater for fills, stutters, and controlled rhythmic disruption.
- **Erosion** (partial; 20 evidence videos): Create harmonics, nonlinear movement, or deliberate degradation.
- **Redux2** (partial; 15 evidence videos): Create harmonics, nonlinear movement, or deliberate degradation.
- **Vinyl** (partial; 9 evidence videos): Create harmonics, nonlinear movement, or deliberate degradation.
- **Corpus** (complete; 23 evidence videos): A resonating physical model that imposes pipe, plate, string, membrane, and related resonances on audio.
- **OriginalSimpler** (complete; 49 evidence videos): A focused sample instrument for classic, one-shot, slicing, and warped sample playback.
