# Mix bus and translation

Preserve the composition's hierarchy while adding cohesion, controlling peaks, and verifying spectral/stereo translation.

## Build order

1. Fix arrangement and channel balance first.
2. Gain-stage into the bus chain consistently.
3. Apply broad tonal or dynamic changes at matched loudness.
4. Use limiting for peak control, not to invent punch.
5. Check mono, low playback level, and multiple references.

## Listen for

- Lead and kick hierarchy survives
- No chronic gain reduction
- Low end remains centered
- Processing helps at matched loudness

## Devices

- **GlueCompressor** (partial; 35 evidence videos): A bus compressor for cohesive envelope control, punch, and sidechain-driven movement.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **Saturator** (partial; 52 evidence videos): A focused waveshaping stage for harmonic density, clipping, warmth, and peak control.
- **Limiter** (partial; 55 evidence videos): Control or exaggerate the amplitude envelope.
- **SpectrumAnalyzer** (complete; 0 evidence videos): A non-coloring spectrum tap for checking frequency balance and resonances.
- **StereoGain** (partial; 50 evidence videos): Ableton's utility/gain family for level, width, channel, phase, and low-frequency stereo control.
