# Drum bus

Turn separate hits into one energetic instrument while preserving transient hierarchy and headroom.

## Build order

1. Balance individual hits first.
2. Remove cumulative low/harsh buildup.
3. Choose one primary bus-color stage.
4. Set bus dynamics from groove timing.
5. Parallelize heavy color if the dry transient must survive.

## Listen for

- Kick remains dominant
- Snare does not collapse
- Hats do not trigger excessive gain reduction
- Matched-level processing adds cohesion

## Devices

- **DrumGroupDevice** (partial; 50 evidence videos): Represent Ableton's serial, parallel, layered, return, selector, and macro graph structure.
- **DrumBuss** (partial; 24 evidence videos): An integrated drum color, transient, compression, and low-frequency enhancement processor.
- **GlueCompressor** (partial; 35 evidence videos): A bus compressor for cohesive envelope control, punch, and sidechain-driven movement.
- **Saturator** (partial; 52 evidence videos): A focused waveshaping stage for harmonic density, clipping, warmth, and peak control.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **Limiter** (partial; 55 evidence videos): Control or exaggerate the amplitude envelope.
