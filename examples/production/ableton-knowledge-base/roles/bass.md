# Bass and low end

Create a mono-stable fundamental with rhythmic definition and controlled upper harmonics that survives different playback systems.

## Build order

1. Write the bass rhythm around kick occupancy.
2. Establish the clean fundamental and note envelope.
3. Add a separate or band-limited harmonic layer.
4. Control peaks and sidechain interaction.
5. Audit mono and small-speaker translation.

## Listen for

- Pitch remains readable
- Kick and bass alternate or interlock
- Sub is centered
- Upper harmonics do not buzz continuously

## Devices

- **Operator** (partial; 46 evidence videos): A four-oscillator FM/additive/subtractive synth suited to precise bass, percussion, keys, and metallic timbres.
- **Drift** (partial; 27 evidence videos): A subtractive synth designed for organic oscillator instability, per-voice variation, and fast modulation.
- **UltraAnalog** (complete; 79 evidence videos): Ableton's analog-modeling subtractive synthesizer family.
- **OriginalSimpler** (complete; 49 evidence videos): A focused sample instrument for classic, one-shot, slicing, and warped sample playback.
- **Roar** (partial; 32 evidence videos): A multi-stage distortion and feedback sound-design system with routing, modulation, filtering, compression, and time behavior.
- **Saturator** (partial; 52 evidence videos): A focused waveshaping stage for harmonic density, clipping, warmth, and peak control.
- **Compressor2** (partial; 100 evidence videos): Control or exaggerate the amplitude envelope.
- **StereoGain** (partial; 50 evidence videos): Ableton's utility/gain family for level, width, channel, phase, and low-frequency stereo control.
- **Eq8** (partial; 38 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
