# Lead, pluck, and hook

Make a memorable foreground voice with intentional articulation, spectral focus, movement, and phrase-aware delay/reverb.

## Build order

1. Write a motif with an answer and a rest pattern.
2. Set note envelope and filter articulation.
3. Add one identity-producing modulation route.
4. Create perspective with filtered delay/reverb.
5. Automate timbre or throws at phrase boundaries.

## Listen for

- Recognizable in dry mono
- Clear attack
- Space fills gaps rather than obscuring notes
- Variation across sections

## Devices

- **Drift** (partial; 27 evidence videos): A subtractive synth designed for organic oscillator instability, per-voice variation, and fast modulation.
- **InstrumentMeld** (partial; 14 evidence videos): A dual-engine macro-oscillator synth for complex timbral blending and modulation.
- **Operator** (partial; 46 evidence videos): A four-oscillator FM/additive/subtractive synth suited to precise bass, percussion, keys, and metallic timbres.
- **UltraAnalog** (complete; 79 evidence videos): Ableton's analog-modeling subtractive synthesizer family.
- **AutoFilter** (partial; 43 evidence videos): Reshape frequency content or move it over time.
- **Chorus2** (partial; 62 evidence videos): Animate pitch, phase, level, or stereo position.
- **Echo** (partial; 53 evidence videos): A character delay with modulation, filtering, stereo modes, reverb, and feedback-path sound design.
- **Reverb** (partial; 145 evidence videos): Place sound in depth or create time-based rhythmic structure.
